Would go on: Probate & Estates page How long it usually takes

From the first weeks to closing

A typical Florida formal administration, start to finish. Many take six to twelve months; yours may be shorter or longer.

  1. First weeksDeath certificate and the will

    Order certified copies. Find the original will and get it to the clerk.

  2. Weeks 1 to 4Secure the home

    Insurance told, locks checked, utilities kept on.

  3. Month 1 to 2The estate is opened

    Your attorney files with the county court.

  4. Often month 2 to 3Letters issued

    The court names the personal representative, who can now sign.

  5. Three months or moreCreditor period

    Runs from the first published notice. The home can often be listed now.

  6. Month 6 to 12Sale and closing

    About 30 to 60 days from contract to closing. Proceeds may wait for the estate to settle.
