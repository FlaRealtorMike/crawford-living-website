Value

More than one number, for different jobs

Date-of-death value
May be needed for the estate’s inventory or taxes. Your attorney or accountant says whether a formal appraisal is required, and for what date.
Today’s market picture
A comparative market analysis from recent nearby sales, the current competition, and the home’s real condition. The one that helps a family decide.
A formal appraisal
An independent value from a licensed appraiser, when one is required.
Condition

As-is, or repaired?

Deferred maintenance, dated finishes, and a house still full of belongings all affect what a home sells for and how buyers receive it.

Before the estate spends money on repairs, it is worth comparing the likely as-is value against the return those repairs would bring. Sometimes the honest answer is to do nothing.

The choices

Keep it, transfer it, rent it, or sell it

Once the home’s legal status and who decides are clear, the family can weigh the real options.

  • Keeping or transferring it raises who takes title, and who carries the mortgage and the costs.
  • Renting it can preserve the home and bring in income, but it creates ongoing responsibilities, and a question of whether the estate has authority to lease.
  • Selling it brings back the timing questions: who signs, and whether the court has to approve.

The best decision is rarely about price alone. None of it has to be settled in a hurry.
