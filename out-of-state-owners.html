The out-of-state checklist

Four questions worth answering early

Most of these take a phone call to settle. Left alone, any one of them can get expensive.

  • 01 · InsuranceDoes the policy still fit an empty house?

    Many homeowners policies limit coverage once a home has been vacant 30 to 60 days. Call the insurer and ask.

  • 02 · SecurityWho's checking on it?

    An empty house shows it: mail piling up, an overgrown yard, no lights at night. A regular check keeps small problems small.

  • 03 · WeatherIs it ready for storm season?

    June through November, someone local should be able to secure shutters, clear gutters, and look for damage afterward.

  • 04 · BillsAre the utilities, taxes, and HOA dues covered?

    Keep power and water on so the house stays dry and cool, and make sure nothing lapses while the family decides.
