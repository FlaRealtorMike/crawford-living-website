About

I've been on the other side of the builder's table.

A Florida-licensed real estate broker of more than twenty years, working across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake and Volusia counties.

Before real estate, my work was coordinating residential construction — scheduling trades, holding subcontractors to dates, and getting houses finished. That background is the reason I'm useful on the kind of transaction where a property needs work: I know how a build is supposed to run, which trades need to be scheduled in what order, and how to get real quotes in front of you quickly. That's process knowledge, not structural expertise — for anything technical you want a licensed inspector or engineer, and I'll tell you which one to call.

I'm also a NAR-certified Real Estate Negotiation Expert. In estate work particularly, a fair amount of the job is helping people who don't fully agree with each other arrive at a decision they can each live with.

Why the practice is small

Crawford Living takes on fewer transactions than it could, by design. The trade-off is real — less volume — and the return is that the person you spoke with first is the person who shows up at the property, and the one negotiating on your behalf.

Real Estate . . . Considered is meant literally. Most of the decisions in this business are better for being slept on, and very little of what I do benefits from being rushed.

Where I work

Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake and Volusia counties — Orlando and the communities around it. That's a deliberate boundary. Local knowledge stops being worth much when it's stretched too thin.

All five sit inside Stellar MLS, so the whole footprint is one market to work rather than five separate ones.

The name

Crawford Living covers all of it: probate and estate property, ordinary resale, and new construction. It isn't a niche brand pretending the rest doesn't exist.

The tagline is meant literally too. Considered, in the sense of thought about properly before anyone signs anything.
