About

Twenty-two years, and I still don't rush anyone into a decision.

I've been a licensed Florida broker since 2004, working Central Florida. RENE and CPRES-certified, and I take on fewer transactions than I could, by design.

I've spent over twenty years doing this, and the parts of the job that matter haven't changed: reading a contract carefully, not letting anyone get rushed into a decision, and staying reachable when something needs an answer. I hold the RENE and CPRES certifications, which both come down to the same thing — helping people who don't fully agree with each other get to a decision they can live with.

One more thing I bring: I came into real estate already knee-deep in residential construction — coordinating trades, holding subcontractors to dates — which is still useful on a house that needs work. I'm not the one doing that work myself, though: for anything technical, you want a licensed inspector or engineer, and I'll tell you exactly who to call.

Crawford Living takes on fewer transactions than it could, by design. The return is more time on each one.
