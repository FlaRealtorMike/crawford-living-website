Oviedo

Its own small city in Seminole County, near UCF, with a walkable park around Center Lake, a long paved trail, and a historic downtown.

Orientation

Short answer. Oviedo is its own small city in Seminole County, near UCF, with a walkable park around Center Lake and the Cross Seminole Trail.

Oviedo sits in southeastern Seminole County, with Lake Jesup on its northern edge, about 10 to 15 minutes from the University of Central Florida. It's its own city of about 40,000 people, with its own downtown, parks and hospital.

The homes

Most of Oviedo grew up as suburban neighborhoods: single-family homes on a range of lot sizes, many in communities with pools and parks. Near the center, Oviedo on the Park adds newer townhomes and homes in a mixed-use setting — about 800 homes on 108 acres. Some older homes sit near the historic downtown.

Daily life

Oviedo on the Park is built around Center Lake Park, with a boardwalk, an amphitheater, a splash pad and a dog park, plus concerts and movie nights. The Cross Seminole Trail runs through town — about 28 miles end to end. The historic downtown is known for its free-roaming chickens.

Getting around

Oviedo connects to the toll road on the east side of the region, which helps with trips to the airport (about 30 minutes) and the UCF area. Downtown Orlando is a longer drive than from the central neighborhoods, so test your commute at the hour you'd really make it.

Before you buy here

Check the HOA rules and fees, and look at the tax bill for any CDD. Check the flood zone, especially near lakes and low areas, and get an insurance quote early. Ask the city what's planned nearby, since new development can change traffic and views. School zones are set by address — confirm with Seminole County Public Schools.

Price ranges

Townhomes about $280K to $370K. Single-family median about $500K to $525K. Some communities $650K to $1M+.
