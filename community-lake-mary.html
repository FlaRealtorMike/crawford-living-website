Lake Mary

A Seminole County city north of Orlando along I-4, with a small downtown, parks and trails, and a business district where many people work close to home.

Orientation

Short answer. Lake Mary is a Seminole County city north of Orlando with a small downtown, parks and trails, and Colonial TownPark. The question most buyers ask is Lake Mary or Heathrow.

Lake Mary is its own city in Seminole County, north of Orlando along Interstate 4 (ZIP 32746). The name covers more than the city itself, though, and that matters when you buy.

The city

The downtown centers on Central Park and City Hall, with a farmers market, community events, and the Lake Mary Events Center on a lake. Just outside downtown, Colonial TownPark has restaurants, shops and a movie theater, with green space for events. The city also has a SunRail station connecting to downtown Orlando.

The homes

The mix is wide: established neighborhoods with sidewalks and community pools, golf course communities, gated enclaves, newer townhomes and condos near the shopping areas, and larger homes on bigger lots nearby. Start with your priorities — lot size, how close you want to be to the shops, how much yard work you want.

Daily life

A typical week might include the Cross Seminole Trail, a Saturday farmers market, and dinner at TownPark. There are parks and sports fields, and several golf courses a short drive away. A lot of daily life happens close to home.

Lake Mary or Heathrow?

Heathrow, next door, is a separate area, but many Heathrow homes use a Lake Mary mailing address. Some homes marketed as Lake Mary are actually in Heathrow, Sanford, Longwood or unincorporated Seminole County. Look up the exact address with the county — taxes, rules and services can differ.

Before you buy here

Confirm which jurisdiction the home is in. Read the HOA rules and fees, especially in gated and golf communities, and check the tax bill for any CDD. Ask the age of the roof and air conditioning, and get a homeowners insurance quote early. School zones are set by address — confirm with Seminole County Public Schools.

Price ranges

Condos and townhomes about $280K to $390K. Single-family median about $520K to $565K. Heathrow often $650K to $1.4M.
