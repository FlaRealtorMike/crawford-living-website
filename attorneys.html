For probate attorneys

Who handles everything the property throws off?

The cleanouts, the repairs, the keys, the heirs with questions. Right now, you do.

That's where I come in. I'm Mike Crawford, a Florida-licensed real estate broker of more than twenty years with a background that blends residential construction and real estate.

Before real estate my work was coordinating residential construction, so I know how to schedule the right subcontractors and vendors to get a property from unmarketable to closing — a skill most agents simply don't have. That is coordination rather than structural assessment; anything technical goes to a licensed inspector or engineer. I'm also a NAR-certified Real Estate Negotiation Expert.

Together that means I can navigate the real estate side, manage the physical property, and keep people talking when they don't agree — in one place, instead of your client coordinating three different people.

What that looks like in practice

  • Project management, start to finish

    Whether a property needs heavy lifting or just a little cleanup, keeping it on track and moving toward closing.
