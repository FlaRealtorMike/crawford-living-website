Resources

Local help for families settling an estate.

Grief support, help with the contents of a house, free legal aid and other local services in Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake and Volusia counties.

Grief support

Free bereavement programs, including for families whose loved one was not in hospice care.

  • Cornerstone Hospice bereavement services: individual and group grief support, open to anyone in the community. Orange, Osceola, Lake · 888-728-6234
  • Halifax Health Hospice bereavement: grief programs for adults and children. Volusia · 386-425-4738

Funeral homes with grief resources

Many local funeral homes keep resource pages for families after a loss.

  • Postell’s Mortuary: grief resources for Orlando families: groups, counseling and local starting points. Orange · 407-295-3857
  • Newcomer Funeral Homes: grief resources; chapels in East Orlando, Winter Park and Longwood. Orange, Seminole · 407-277-4227
  • Golden’s Funeral Home: grief support articles for families. Orange (Winter Park) · 407-740-6784
  • DeGusipe Funeral Home: grief support; chapels in Maitland, Sanford and Ocoee. Orange, Seminole · 407-695-2273
  • Ganderson Brothers Mortuary: a year of grief support messages. Seminole (Sanford) · 321-363-0529
  • Becker Funeral Home: a local grief support directory. Lake (Clermont) · 352-394-7121
  • Allen-Summerhill Funeral Homes: grief support links. Volusia (DeLand, Orange City)
  • Woodward Funeral Homes: grief resources. Volusia (Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach) · 386-253-7601
  • Osceola Memory Gardens: resources for navigating a loss. Osceola (Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Poinciana) · 407-847-2494

Estate sales, cleanouts and senior moves

Help with what is inside the house, before or after it sells.

  • Kinfolk Estate Sales: family-owned estate sales and downsizing help. Lake, west Orange · 352-557-9771
  • Settled In by Tina: senior move management, from sorting to setting up the new home. Orange, Seminole, Lake, Volusia · 407-575-0531
  • Caring Transitions of Winter Park: estate sales, downsizing and relocation. Orange, Seminole · 407-543-2273
  • Interiors for Seniors: downsizing moves for older adults. Orange (Apopka) · 321-279-3301
  • Dennison Estate Clean Out: estate sales and full cleanouts. Orange · 407-773-1447

Money, paperwork and care decisions

  • Life After Grief Financial Planning: fee-only financial planning for people after a loss, with a free resource list. Orange, and virtual · 407-917-1913
  • Florida Daily Money Management: help with bills, mail and household paperwork. Orange · 407-768-4068
  • Orlando Senior Advisor: free help comparing assisted living, memory care and in-home care. Orange, Osceola, Seminole · 689-400-3031

Free legal help

For income-eligible residents. For any legal question about an estate, talk with a Florida attorney.

  • Legal Aid Society of the Orange County Bar Association: free civil legal help. Orange · 407-841-8310
  • Seminole County Bar Association Legal Aid Society: free civil legal help. Seminole
  • Community Legal Services of Mid-Florida: free civil legal aid, including wills and estates. Central Florida · 800-405-1417
