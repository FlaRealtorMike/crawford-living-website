The Marketing Menu

Most agents sell one bundle. This is a menu.

A downtown condo and a lakefront home in Windermere sell to different people. A condo buyer scrolls photos on a phone at lunch. A lakefront buyer wants to see the dock from the air before booking a showing. Your marketing should be built for the buyer most likely to pay the most for your house.

On every listing

Included, whatever the price.

  • Professional photography, shot and edited for the listing. The photos are what get a buyer to book the showing.
  • Full MLS exposure. Stellar MLS sends your listing to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin and hundreds of brokerage sites on day one.
  • A prep walkthrough before it goes live: what to fix, what to leave, and how to set up the rooms buyers care about most.
  • Yard sign and open houses, for the neighbors, the drive-by buyers, and the people who want to stand in the kitchen before they call.
  • Social media posts at launch, for each open house, and for every status change.

Add-ons you choose

Matched to your buyer. Priced with you before anything is spent.

  • Video walkthrough, for homes where the flow and finishes do the selling.
  • Drone photos and video, for water, views, big lots, golf frontage, or a neighborhood that’s part of the price.
  • 3D tour and floor plan, for out-of-state buyers who make their shortlist before they fly in.
  • Professional staging, in person or virtual. Virtually staged photos are labeled, as MLS rules require.
  • Targeted ads on Facebook, Instagram and Google, aimed by location, price range and interest.
  • Flyers and just-listed mailers to the neighbors, who often know the next buyer.
