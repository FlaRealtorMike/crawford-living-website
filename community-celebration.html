Communities

Celebration

A town planned from scratch in the 1990s, with front porches, brick streets and a walkable downtown on a lake. Disney no longer runs it, but the look and the rules remain.

Orientation

Celebration is in unincorporated Osceola County (ZIP 34747), a few miles from the theme parks. The first residents moved in during June 1996; about 11,000 people lived there as of the 2020 Census.

How it started

Disney planned Celebration using traditional small-town design. It sold the downtown commercial district in 2004 and stepped back from running the community. Today the neighborhoods are managed by a residents' association and village associations, and Osceola County provides services such as zoning.

The homes

Homes follow a pattern book — design guidelines that limit exterior styles to Colonial, Victorian, Craftsman, Coastal, Mediterranean and a few others. Front porches sit close to the sidewalk, and garages are usually in back, off an alley. There are condos, townhomes and single-family homes across several villages, each with its own character.

Daily life

The town center has a brick-paved main street, restaurants and shops, and a promenade along Lake Rianhard. Miles of trails link the villages, along with neighborhood parks and pools. Visitors like it too, so the town center gets busy.

Getting around

Celebration connects to the toll roads, so the theme parks, the airport and the rest of Orlando are reachable without relying on I-4. Expect more traffic near the town center and on busy theme park days.

Before you buy here

The pattern book keeps Celebration looking the way it does, and it also means rules. Read the association documents and ask what's allowed on the outside of the home — paint, fences, landscaping. Ask what the fees are and what they cover, and read the rental rules if you might ever rent. School zones are set by address — confirm with the School District of Osceola County.

Price ranges

Condos from the $100Ks to $200Ks. Townhomes about $570K. Single-family median about $600K to $870K, depending on the source. Estates $1M+.
