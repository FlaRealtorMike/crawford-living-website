Why the first price matters

The market tells you either way.

  • Priced too high, the listing sits, the price gets cut, and buyers start asking what’s wrong with it.
  • Priced too low, you leave money on the table before the first showing.
  • Priced right, the only negotiating left is the good kind.
How the number gets built
  1. 01Recent closed sales near youfrom the last few months. A comp from a year ago is already stale.
  2. 02Adjusted for real differencesbetween those houses and yours: size, condition, lot and updates.
  3. 03Weighed against your goal.A fast sale and the top dollar call for different prices.
