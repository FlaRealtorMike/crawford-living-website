Where it's filed

Where probate is filed in Lake County

Probate is filed in the county where the person lived. For Lake County, that is the Lake County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller, in the Fifth Judicial Circuit. The clerk's probate office is at the Lake County Courthouse at 550 W. Main Street in Tavares, East Wing. The main number is 352-742-4100.

The clerk's records search shows the case once it is filed. Lake County is in the Fifth Judicial Circuit, which is a different circuit from Orange, Osceola and Seminole.

An attorney files the case for most families. If there is no attorney yet, the first call is to a Florida probate attorney, not to a real estate agent. Once there is one, I work to the attorney's timeline.

Who owns it

How to look up the owner, the homestead and the deed

The Lake County Property Appraiser website shows the owner of record, whether the home has a homestead exemption, and the last recorded transfer. The clerk's official records show the recorded deed, any mortgage and any lien. Together they answer the first question a family has: whose name is on the home, and does it need probate to move?

A home held in a trust, under a lady bird deed, or jointly with survivorship often passes without a probate case. A home titled only in the name of the person who died generally does not. The Central Florida inherited-house guide walks through each case.

What tends to matter for homes in Lake County

Lake County runs from the Clermont area, which borders Winter Garden and Horizon West, to lake towns like Mount Dora and Tavares. Homes here range from in-town homes near a historic downtown to large lots outside city limits, and acreage, wells and septic systems can add steps that a typical suburban sale does not have.

Common questions

Inherited a house in Lake County: common questions

Where is probate filed for a home in Lake County?

In the county where the person lived when they died. For Lake County that is the Lake County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller, part of the Fifth Judicial Circuit. Florida has no separate probate court; probate is a division of the circuit court.

How do I find out who owns a Lake County home and whether it has homestead?

The Lake County Property Appraiser website shows the owner of record, the homestead status and the last recorded transfer. The clerk's official records show the recorded deed and any mortgage or lien.

Can the home be sold before the probate case is finished?

Often, once the court has appointed a personal representative, and sometimes with a court order for the sale. If the home passed outside probate, probate may not be needed. The family's attorney confirms which applies.

Can a family member who lives out of state handle this?

Florida limits who can serve as a personal representative when they live out of state. The attorney confirms who qualifies. Either way, someone local can check the home, collect mail and keep the utilities on while the court process runs.
