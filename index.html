Some houses are sold. Others have to be untangled first.

Twenty years of Florida real estate, and before that coordinating residential construction. Both turn out to matter when a property needs work before it can be sold.

I'm Mike Crawford. Crawford Living takes on fewer transactions than it could, by design — the person you speak with first is the person who sees it through to closing.

Much of my work involves property held in an estate: helping personal representatives, and the attorneys who advise them, get a house through probate and sold. It's unhurried work, and it usually arrives at a difficult time for a family.

I also represent buyers and sellers in ordinary resale and in new construction. Estate work is a specialty, not the whole practice.
