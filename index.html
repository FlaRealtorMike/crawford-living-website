Why this is different

Advice you can act on, not just an opinion.

Plenty of agents can tell you what a house is worth. The harder question is what to do about it — and that depends on the property, the timing, and the people involved.

Sequencing

A plan before a listing

Some properties need sequencing, coordination and a few decisions made in the right order before a sale makes sense. Working out that order is part of the job, not an extra.

Numbers

Repair or sell as-is, with numbers

A cost-and-return comparison for the specific property, built on real contractor quotes, so the decision is yours to make on facts. Sometimes the answer is to do nothing.

Negotiation

A certified negotiator

NAR Real Estate Negotiation Expert. A fair amount of this work is helping people who don’t fully agree arrive at a decision they can each live with.

Direct representation

Different situations. The same representation.

Crawford Living takes on fewer transactions than it could, by design. The trade-off is real — less volume — and the return is that nothing gets handed off, and the standard does not change depending on which door you came in through.

  • The person you speak with first is the person who meets you at the property.
  • The same person advises you and negotiates on your behalf.
  • One point of contact, from the first conversation to the closing table.
  • No queue to reach the person responsible for the work.
