Communities

Dr. Phillips

Southwest Orlando's established neighborhoods, golf communities and lakes, with Sand Lake Road's Restaurant Row running through the middle.

Orientation

Short answer. Dr. Phillips is one of southwest Orlando's best-known areas: established neighborhoods, golf communities, lakes and Restaurant Row on Sand Lake Road.

Dr. Phillips is an unincorporated part of Orange County; most homes use an Orlando mailing address (ZIP 32819, and parts of 32836). It sits between Windermere, International Drive and Universal, with I-4 close by. It's named for Dr. Philip Phillips, a citrus grower whose groves once covered much of this land — the performing arts center that shares his name is downtown, not here.

The homes

Built up over many years, the area mixes established single-family neighborhoods, golf course communities, gated communities, lakefront homes, townhomes and condos. Many neighborhoods have mature landscaping. Each community has its own association, so rules and fees depend on the street.

Daily life

Restaurant Row runs along West Sand Lake Road between I-4 and Apopka-Vineland Road, with more than two dozen restaurants. There's a 43-acre county park on Big Sand Lake, golf, and access to the Butler Chain of Lakes along the western edge. Groceries, medical offices and shopping are close by.

Getting around

It's a car-based area with quick access to I-4 and the toll roads, close to the convention center, the theme parks and the airport corridor. Sand Lake Road can back up at rush hour and on busy tourist weekends, so try the drive at the times you'd really make it.

Before you buy here

Read the HOA rules and fees for that specific community, and find out how access works if it's gated. Check the flood zone, especially near lakes, and get an insurance quote early. If you might ever rent the home out, read the rental rules — many communities limit it. School zones are set by address — confirm with Orange County Public Schools.

Price ranges

Most single-family about $550K to $1.8M. Luxury $2M+.
