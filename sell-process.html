What happens, in order

Five stages, no surprises.

  1. Before listingPlan and price

    Your goal, the price and the prep list, plus a written marketing plan.

  2. While it’s liveHonest updates

    Who looked, what they said, and what it means for the price. A quiet week is information too.

  3. Offers arriveRead every offer

    Financing, timeline and likely repairs, not price alone. The offer that closes wins.

  4. Often 30 to 45 daysUnder contract

    A transaction coordinator keeps dates and documents moving. Judgment calls come to me early.

  5. Closing dayKeys and proceeds

    The title company runs the closing. You sign, hand over the keys, and get paid.

Negotiation

The better offer isn’t always the bigger number.

I’m a certified Real Estate Negotiation Expert (RENE). The work is negotiating the whole offer: price, financing, timeline and repairs, so what you agree to is what actually closes.
